Mint Brownies

★★★★★

Dessert

Ingredients

1 1/2 cup butter (3 sticks + 6T as written)

2 cups sugar

2 T vanilla

4 eggs

3/4 cup Hershey cocoa

1 cup flour

1/2 t baking powder

2 2/3 cups powdered sugar

1 T + 1 t water

1 t mint extract

4 drops green color

1 cup Hershey chips

Directions

Heat oven to 350. Use 13x9 pan.

Brownie base:
Melt 2 sticks butter. Add granulated sugar, vanilla, eggs, cocoa powder, flour & baking powder. Bake for 30-35min. Cool on wire rack.

Mint layer:
Mix powdered sugar, 1 stick butter, water, mint, food coloring. Spread on brownies. Cool until hard.

Chocolate layer:
Mix 6T butter & chocolate chips in microwave to melt. Pour over brownies. Cover. Cool >1hr before serving.
