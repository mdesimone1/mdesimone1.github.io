Costco Quinoa Salad (Copycat Recipe)

Prep Time: 15 minutes Cook Time: 15 minutes Servings: Yield: 4-6 servings Source: letsdishrecipes.com

Ingredients

FOR THE DRESSING:

1/4 cup olive oil

1 tablespoon white wine vinegar

1 tablespoon lemon juice

1 tablespoon sugar

Salt and pepper, to taste

FOR THE SALAD:

2 cups cooked, cooled quinoa

1 cup canned lentils, rinsed and drained

1 red pepper, seeded and diced

1 small tomato, seeded and diced

1 medium cucumber, diced

1 cup kale, finely chopped

2-3 tablespoons freshly chopped cilantro

Directions

In a large bowl, whisk together the ingredients for the dressing.

Add all of the salad ingredients to the bowl and toss to mix well. Season with salt and pepper, to taste.

Serve at room temperature, or refrigerate until ready to serve.
