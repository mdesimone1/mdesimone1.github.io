Nut Granola Parfait

Prep Time: 20 min Cook Time: 1 hr 5 min Difficulty: Easy Servings: Yield: 1 serving Source: foodnetwork.com

Ingredients

1 cup vanilla Greek yogurt

2 tablespoons honey

1/2 cup Nut Granola, recipe follows

Fresh mint leaves, blueberries and raspberries, for garnish


Nut Granola:

1/3 cup maple syrup

3 tablespoons coconut oil

1 tablespoon vanilla bean paste

1 mounded teaspoon ground cinnamon

1 cup chopped raw cashews

1 cup raw pecan pieces

1 cup raw pumpkin seeds

1/2 cup raw sunflower seeds

1/4 cup sesame seeds

1 cup unsweetened flaked coconut

Sea salt, for sprinkling over the top

Directions

Add 1/2 cup yogurt to a parfait glass or mason jar, then drizzle with 1 tablespoon honey and top with 1/4 cup Granola. Repeat the layers once more, then garnish with the mint leaves and berries.

Preheat the oven to 325 degrees F.

Mix the maple syrup, coconut oil, vanilla bean paste and cinnamon in a large bowl.

Add the nuts, seeds and flaked coconut, tossing them to fully coat. Spread in an even layer on a sheet pan and bake until golden, 25 to 30 minutes. Sprinkle the sea salt over the top, then let cool. Break into pieces.

Place in an airtight container, where it will keep for up to 1 week.
