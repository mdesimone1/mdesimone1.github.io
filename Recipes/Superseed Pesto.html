Superseed Pesto

Prep Time: 10 mins Cook Time: 10 mins Servings: Serves: 1 ½ cups Source: blissfulbasil.com

Ingredients

⅓ cup hemp seeds

⅓ cup raw pepitas (pumpkin seeds)

2 cups loosely packed basil leaves

1 cup arugula

1 garlic clove

1 tablespoon fresh lemon juice

¼ cup olive oil

½ teaspoon sea salt

black pepper, to taste

Directions

Add all ingredients to a food processor and process until smooth (approximately 2-3 minutes).

Slather on pasta, pizza, sandwiches, and veggies.

Store leftovers in refrigerator.
