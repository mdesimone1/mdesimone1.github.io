Margooga

★★★★★

Source: Culinary magic of the emirates by Alexandra von Hahn

Ingredients

1 kg whole chicken cut into 8-10 pieces

⅓ cup vegetable oil

2 onions, peeled and diced

1 large potato, peeled and cubed

3 medium carrots, peeled and sliced

3 large tomatoes, diced

1 loumi (dried lime), crushed

1 tbsp salt / tsp chilli powder

1 tsp ginger, crushed

½ tsp saffron threads

1 Tablespoon ceusjed garlic

2 tablespoon bzar spice mix

1 cup ciñantro chopped for garnish


for bread:

3 c flour

½ tsp salt

¾ c lulewarm water, up to 1 cup

Directions

MARGOOGA
Rub chicken with ½ tsp bzar and leave for 30 minutes in refrigerator or until ready to cook. (Suggest at least 1tbsp is needed)

Fry onions in oil until transparent
Add the rest of the bzar (suggest 2-3 tsp), garlic, ginger, chilli, and cook until spices become fragrant.

Add chicken and cook over moderate heat until chicken begins to colour, about 10 mins
Add tomatoes, loumi, ½ cup of water and saffron and mix well over moderate heat.

Cover pan and cook until spices become fragrant.

Add cubed / sliced vegetables, 6 cups of water and salt, stir well and bring to a boil.

Cover and simmer until water is reduced by half and chicken and vegetables are cooked.

Carefully drop soft bread squares into hot chicken stew

BREAD
Put flour and add salt in a bowl.
Slowly add lukewarm water and stir.
Knead and mix with your fingers until the dough becomes smooth and elastic.

Roll gently on a floured surface. The dough should reach a thickness of about ¼ cm. Use pasta rolller if avail on 2nd largest.

Cover with a tea towel and leave the dough to dry. This process may take a few hours. Dough should be dry on the surface. Cut dried dough in 4 cm x 4 cm squares. This bread for margooga stays unbaked.
