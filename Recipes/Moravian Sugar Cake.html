Moravian Sugar Cake

Prep Time: 30 mins Cook Time: bake the cake at 375°f for 18 to 20 minutes Servings: 9 Source: southernliving.com

Ingredients

Dough:

1 small (6 oz.) russet potato, peeled and chopped (about 1 1/4 cups)

1/4 cup water (100°F to 110°F)

1 (1/4-oz.) envelope active dry yeast

1/3 cup, plus 1/4 tsp. granulated sugar, divided

6 Tbsp. (3 oz.) unsalted butter, melted and cooled

1 large egg, lightly beaten

3/4 tsp. kosher salt

1 1/2 to 2 cups (about 6 3/8 oz. to 8 1/2 oz.) all-purpose flour

Cooking spray

Topping:

1/2 cup packed light brown sugar

1/2 tsp. ground cinnamon

1/4 cup unsalted butter, melted and cooled

Directions

Cook the potatoes:

Place chopped potato in a small saucepan, and cover with cold water. Bring to a simmer over medium; cook, undisturbed, until potato is tender, about 10 minutes.

Drain and mash the potatoes:

Drain potato, and return to saucepan. Mash potato until very smooth. Measure out 1/2 cup mashed potato, and set aside. (Reserve extra potato for another use or discard.)

Activate yeast:

While potato is cooking, stir together warm water, yeast, and 1/4 teaspoon of the sugar in the bowl of a stand mixer. Let stand at room temperature until foamy, 5 to 10 minutes.

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Add mashed potatoes:

Stir in 1/2 cup mashed potato until evenly combined.

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Finish Dough:

Add melted butter, egg, salt, remaining 1/3 cup sugar, and 1 1/2 cups of the flour.

Beat with a stand mixer fitted with a dough hook attachment on low speed until combined, 1 to 2 minutes, scraping down sides of bowl as needed.

Increase speed to medium, and beat until Dough is mostly smooth, about 5 minutes, adding remaining 1/2 cup of flour as needed, 1 tablespoon at a time, to create a soft Dough. (Dough should be soft and tacky but not sticky.)

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Let Dough rise:

Transfer Dough to a large clean bowl coated with cooking spray; turn Dough to coat with cooking spray on all sides. Cover and let rise at room temperature until nearly doubled in volume, 60 to 90 minutes. Coat a 9-inch square baking pan with cooking spray.

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Add Dough to pan:

Punch down Dough, and transfer to prepared pan, gently pressing Dough into an even layer, and pushing Dough into corners of pan.

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Cover and let rise at room temperature until puffy, 30 to 45 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat oven to 375°F.

CAITLIN BENSEL; FOOD STYLIST: TORIE COX

Stir together brown sugar and cinnamon in a small bowl. Indent Dough using your knuckles, and sprinkle evenly with cinnamon-sugar mixture.

Drizzle melted butter evenly over top.

Bake cake:

Bake in preheated oven until puffed and golden brown, 15 to 18 minutes. Let cool in pan on a wire rack for 1 hour. Cut into 9 squares, and serve.
